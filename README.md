# The Circle of Life — 120-Second Procedural Cinema

A 100% procedural 120-second (`2,880` frames @ `24 FPS`, `1280×720`) cinematic short film and synchronized stereo binaural soundtrack built entirely in pure Python (`moviepy`, `numpy`, `opencv-python`, `scipy`) with zero external assets.

- **Direct MP4 Download:** https://raw.githubusercontent.com/catalystberry842-alt/the-circle-of-life-120s/main/the_circle_of_life_120s.mp4
- **Python Pipeline:** [`the_circle_of_life.py`](https://github.com/catalystberry842-alt/the-circle-of-life-120s/blob/main/the_circle_of_life.py)

## 7-Act Structure
1. **Act I `[0:00–0:15]` Genesis & Rain** — Bioluminescent cyan L-system mycelium, pink-noise rain, concentric water ripples & downward-sweep drop synths.
2. **Act II `[0:15–0:30]` The Flora** — Sunrise volumetric god rays, Fibonacci spiral unrolling fractal alien fern, golden pollen, major-chord ambient shift.
3. **Act III `[0:30–0:50]` The Armored Consumer** — Smooth-min SDF Rhinoceros Beetle with emerald-to-violet view-angle Iridescence, 6-leg analytical IK & clicky square-wave foley.
4. **Act IV `[0:50–1:10]` The Camouflage** — Camera pan right, Voronoi cellular bark-to-red/blue camouflage reveal, SSS flesh glow, independent turret eyes & motion-blurred ballistic tongue strike.
5. **Act V `[1:10–1:30]` Thermal Vision (The Viper)** — Dark canopy drop into full-frame FLIR Thermal Heatmap Vision, heat-signature SDF Viper drop/coil, dissonant drone & high-frequency hiss.
6. **Act VI `[1:30–1:50]` The Apex Dive** — Golden Hour sunset, directional feather-noise Golden Eagle sheer vertical dive, 1.0s total silence, white-noise impact explosion, camera shake & 3D feather burst toward the lens.
7. **Act VII `[1:50–2:00]` Dust to Dust** — Nightfall, single feather landing on wet soil, cyan bioluminescent fungi blooming out of its quill, returning gentle rain & fade to black.
